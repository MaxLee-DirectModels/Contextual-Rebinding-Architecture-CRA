# CRA v1.1 Structural Lexicon

A categorized, lookup-only vocabulary sidecar for deeper analysis, predecessor mapping, profile discovery, and terminology audits.

> **Non-sovereignty rule:** Naming or matching a term never activates a CRA root, operator, profile, guarantee, Judgment, Standing, authority, or Effect.

The normative architecture remains `references/cra.json`. Aliases are retrieval handles, not automatic equivalences. Equal spelling across Regimes does not establish equal meaning.

## Status key

- **CORE** — exact CRA kernel or operator vocabulary; resolve through its normative destination.
- **SUPPORT** — activate only when the Demand and operator make the distinction result-changing.
- **PROFILE** — activate only through a named, currently qualified profile cell.
- **APPLICATION** — activate only inside a named external Regime, Demand, Basis, and verifier contract.
- **OPEN_FRONTIER** — conserved release obligation; still open until its external closure predicate is met.
- **SUPERSEDED** — historical sense that must be decomposed into current terms before use.
- **RETIRED** — lineage-only vocabulary that must not reactivate its old architecture.
